# 📲 How to Connect a Bot to FeedsGram

Connecting your own Telegram bot to FeedsGram is quick and easy. Just follow the steps below:

## Step 1: Create a New Bot with [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather)

1. Open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) on Telegram.
2. Send the command `/newbot` to start the bot creation process.
3. Follow the prompts:
   - Give your bot a **name** (e.g., *My Feedback Bot*).
   - Choose a unique **username** (it must end in `bot`, e.g., *my_feedback_bot*).

Once done, BotFather will send you a **bot token** — a long string of characters that looks like this: >123456789:ABCDefGhIJKlmNoPQRsTuvWXyZ123456789

This token is essential — it allows FeedsGram to connect and manage your bot.

---

## Step 2: Share the Bot Token with FeedsGram

There are two ways to send the bot token to FeedsGram:

- **Option 1: Forward the token message**  
  Simply forward the message you received from BotFather directly into this chat.

- **Option 2: Copy and Paste the Token**  
  Copy the token text and paste it here in the chat.

FeedsGram will automatically detect the token and begin connecting your bot.

---

## Step 3: Done!

After successfully sharing your token, FeedsGram will set everything up for you — no technical skills required.
