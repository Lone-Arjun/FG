# 💼 Feedsgram Pricing Plans

Feedsgram offers flexible pricing to suit businesses of all sizes — from individuals just starting out to enterprises with advanced needs. Below are our available plans:

---

## 🆓 Free Forever Plan

Perfect for individuals or small teams testing out Telegram bot automation.

- 💸 **Price**: ₹0/month
- 🤖 **Bots**: Up to 3 bots per account
- 🌍 **Users & Requests**: Unlimited
- 👥 **Group Integrations**: 3
- 📁 **Customer Tools**: Basic management features
- ✉️ **Support**: Limited email support
- 🔧 **Features**:
  - Access to core Feedsgram features
  - Easy bot setup and command configuration

---

## 💎 Pro Plan *(Most Popular)*

Ideal for growing businesses that need more control, customization, and support.

- 💰 **Price**: ₹39/month
- 🤖 **Bots**: Up to 5 bots per account
- 🌍 **Users & Requests**: Unlimited
- 🔗 **Integrations**: Multi-channel (Telegram groups, webhooks, etc.)
- 🏷️ **Customer Management**:
  - Advanced segmentation
  - Tagging and filtering
- 📊 **Analytics**: Full access to usage reports and customer insights
- 🚑 **Support**: Priority email and in-app chat support
- 🚀 **Other Perks**:
  - Up to 5 group integrations
  - Higher filter/action limits

---

## 🚀 Ultra Plan

Built for high-demand businesses requiring premium features and dedicated support.

- 💵 **Price**: ₹79/month
- 🤖 **Bots**: Up to 8 bots per account
- 🌍 **Users & Requests**: Unlimited
- 🛠️ **Customization**:
  - Full control of user workflows
  - Custom command logic
- ⚡ **Performance**: Fastest processing and responses
- 📞 **Support**:
  - 24/7 dedicated support
  - Priority queue handling
  - Dedicated account manager
- 🚀 **Integrations & Limits**:
  - Higher filtering limits
  - Deep integrations with external systems
